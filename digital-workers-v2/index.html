What is a digital worker?

Not a chatbot. A worker with one job.

A digital worker doesn't answer questions. It completes a task: scoped to one responsibility, configured to your program and accountable for every outcome. Every worker in the catalog is built the same way, so each one you add is as predictable as the last.

  1. One defined job

    Each worker owns a single task, like auditing timecards or screening submissions, and does it the same way every time. No drift between reviewers.

  2. Your policies, applied

    Configured to your confirmation letters, pay rules and credential requirements. Not generic defaults.

  3. Shows its work

    Every decision carries the rule it applied and why. Disputes get an answer, not a search.

  4. Native to Trio VMS

    Runs inside the platform that already manages your program. No bolt-on, no new login, no data leaving the system.
