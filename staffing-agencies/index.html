AI Digital Worker

Submission Evaluator

Know exactly why a candidate fits. Or doesn't.

Submission Evaluator is an AI Digital Worker that reads every submission against the job's requirements the moment it lands. It returns a job-fit score with the strengths, gaps and reasoning behind it. Where your program shares that feedback, you stop submitting into a black box.

  • See how each candidate stacks up against the job's real requirements.
  • Get the gaps behind the score, not just a number.
  • Sharpen who you put forward on the next similar job.
  • Turn every submission into intel that makes the next one stronger.

Same yardstick for everyone. Every candidate is measured against the same requirements. Scores inform reviewers; people make the call.

Your edge: Fewer misses. More candidates who move forward.

Feature Focus

Job Q&A

Ask once. Everyone gets the answer. Get back to recruiting.

No more chasing a bonus detail through three email threads. Job Q&A puts a shared question-and-answer thread on every job in Trio. When staff answer, every agency on that job sees it at the same time. Same information. Same starting line.

  • Ask job-specific questions right on the job.
  • Search what's already been answered before you ask.
  • Nail down bonuses, schedules, qualifications and policies before you submit.
  • Kill the one-off emails and follow-up calls.
Your edge: Brief candidates right the first time. No surprises after placement.

Feature Focus

Job Competition Scorer

Know where to compete. Win where it counts.

Not every open job deserves the same effort. Job Competition Scorer shows how crowded each opportunity is, so your recruiters stop spreading thin and start going where they can win.

  • See competition levels across open jobs at a glance.
  • Go after the jobs that play to your recruiting strengths.
  • Put recruiters and sourcing effort where they pay off.
  • Spend less time on long shots.
Your edge: Your recruiters' hours go where they're most likely to pay off.
