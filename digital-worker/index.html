01The next workforce isn't just human

Build a digital workforce alongside your human one.

Scale expertise, not headcount.

Healthcare organizations have spent decades building their human workforce. The next evolution is building a digital workforce alongside it.

Trio Digital Workers are specialized workers that take on high-volume, rules-based tasks, like timecard auditing and candidate submission evaluation, to extend your team's operational capacity. They don't replace your workforce; they multiply what it can accomplish, with your people in control of every final decision.

03How a Digital Worker works

Hired like an employee. Governed like one, too.

A Digital Worker isn't a script or a one-off automation. It works the way a new team member does: given a role, handed the rules of the job, and kept accountable to a person. That's what makes it a teammate rather than a tool.

01

Given a role and a job description

Each Digital Worker is defined by a specific job function, what it's responsible for and what falls to a person, just like any role you'd hire for.

02

Operates against your policies and rules

It works from your implementation guidelines, policies, and rules, its handbook, applying them consistently to every case, at enterprise scale.

03

Works with a human in the loop

It surfaces recommendations and the exceptions that need judgment. A person keeps the final word on every decision that matters.

A role you can define. Rules you set. Decisions that stay human.

05Built on trust

The AI surfaces. You decide.

Every Trio Digital Worker is built on the same three principles that govern all Applied AI at Trio. This is the trust spine of the platform.

Security First

Data security and client confidentiality are non-negotiable. We build trust before features, always.

Human in the Loop

Humans define intent and judgment. The Digital Worker executes and surfaces. The human always has the final word on what matters.

Autonomy Is Earned

We move deliberately: recommend, then act under review, then exception-based monitoring. A Digital Worker earns greater autonomy through demonstrated trust.

The autonomy ladder
01

Recommend

The worker proposes; a person approves every action before it takes effect.

02

Act under review

It handles routine cases, with its work reviewed and sampled by your team.

03

Exception-based monitoring

It runs the routine; only edge cases and exceptions surface to a person.

More human oversightMore earned autonomy ↑

A Digital Worker starts by recommending, with a person reviewing its work. As it proves itself, you extend its autonomy at your pace, never ahead of it.
