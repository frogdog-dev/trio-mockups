Brand & Web Style Guide

One brand, formalized. Print to pixel.

This is the single reference for the Trio brand on screen. It pairs the official corporate brand foundation, the logo and its color system, with the digital design language approved on live pages, so print, social, and web finally speak the same language.

01The logo

The mark is fixed. Protect it.

The three-hexagon mark and the "trio Workforce Solutions" wordmark are the corporate signature. Use the supplied artwork exactly, never recreate, recolor, or redraw it.

Primary, full color on white (always preferred).
Reversed, one-color white, for dark or photographic-free dark fields.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep a clear zone around the logo, free of type, imagery, and the edge, at least the height of the hex mark. Minimum size: 1.25″ wide for the primary lockup, 0.55″ for the wide/short form. Retain proportions; never stretch.

Improper uses

1Don't recreate or re-set the type.
2Don't stretch or distort.
3Don't change the colors.
4Don't omit any element.
5Don't add outlines or effects.
6Don't rotate.
7Don't place it inside a shape.
8Don't place it over photographs.
02Color

A brand foundation, and its digital extension.

Two layers, reconciled. The brand foundation is the official corporate palette that governs the logo and print. The digital system is the slightly refined, screen-tuned version the client approved on live pages, brighter contrast, softer neutrals, built for interfaces. Use the digital tokens for the website; treat the foundation as the anchor.

03Typography

Inter, set big, tight, and confident.

One family does the whole system: Inter (fallbacks Avenir Next, Helvetica Neue, Arial). Headlines run heavy with strongly negative letter-spacing; body stays comfortable and readable. The only exception is a decorative Georgia quote glyph.

04Components

05Motion & flair

Life, in service of the point.

The brand earns attention with restrained, purposeful motion, never decoration for its own sake. Every effect illustrates something real, and all of it yields to prefers-reduced-motion so it's accessible by default.

↑
Scroll reveal

Sections rise and fade in as they enter view, quiet, quick, one-time.

#
Count-ups

~
Live data feel

The Market Intelligence panel ticks and re-prices on a gentle random walk, a real, interactive moment.

Traveling signal pulses along the orchestration lines; a soft cursor spotlight on interactive cards.
